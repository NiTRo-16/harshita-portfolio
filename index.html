01 · TEAM PROJECT

Dementia Patient Support Platform

People living with dementia often struggle with memory and daily routines, and the people caring for them carry a lot of that load. This platform aims to make everyday care calmer and more organised for both.

The idea

An accessible digital assistant for routines and care, designed to be simple enough for patients and useful for caregivers.

Important

It's a support tool, not a medical or diagnostic system, and it doesn't replace doctors.

Planned features

  • Daily routine management and gentle reminders
  • Caregiver support and appointment tracking
  • Patient information kept in one place
